Installation Options

Mounting Configurations

Each system can be adapted to your architecture. Choose from freestanding, wall-mounted, or hybrid setups.

Freestanding

Base-plate mounted directly to the floor. No walls needed — perfect for open terraces and pool areas.

Wall-Mounted

Panels fixed to an existing wall with concealed brackets. Clean lines with structural support from the building.

Hybrid

Combination of wall-mount and freestanding posts for L-shaped or U-shaped terrace enclosures.

Recessed Channel

Glass sits inside a flush-mounted channel in the floor. The most minimal option — no visible hardware at all.
